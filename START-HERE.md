# Start here, Maxwell

This is an Obsidian vault set up for two things: your **morning pages** and **your novel**,
world and all. It's a copy of Holden's setup (the parchment look, zen mode, spell check off,
the morning pages hotkey) with a home for chapters, characters and every corner of the world.

## The easy way: let Claude do it

Open the Claude desktop app, go to the **Code** tab, and paste the prompt Holden sent you. Claude
installs Obsidian if needed, puts the vault in your Documents folder, tells you the two or
three things to click, and then brings in your chapters and world-building.

## The by-hand way

1. Download Obsidian from obsidian.md and install it.
2. On this page on GitHub: **Code → Download ZIP**. Unzip it.
3. Move the `vault` folder to `Documents/Obsidian/` and rename it whatever you like.
4. Open Obsidian → **Open folder as vault** → pick that folder.
5. When it asks about plugins, click **Trust author and enable plugins**. Then quit Obsidian
   (Cmd+Q) and open it again.
6. Click the house icon, top left. You're home.

## Once you're in

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| **Cmd+Shift+M** | New morning pages |
| **Cmd+Shift+P** | Zen mode (Esc to leave) |
| **Cmd+O** | Find any note |

The note called **Readme** inside the vault explains everything else: the novel's tables,
canon vs idea, the timeline, and what to ask Claude.

To bring your existing chapters in later: drop the files in the vault's `Inbox` folder, open
Claude Code in the vault folder and say "bring in what's in the Inbox".
