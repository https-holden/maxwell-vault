Julia Cameron's twelve-week course. Two basic tools:

1. **Morning pages**: three pages of stream of consciousness, every morning. **Cmd+Shift+M**. All of them: [[Morning Pages]].
2. **Artist date**: a weekly solo outing to fill the well. Log it in the week's note, or give it its own page from the *Artist's Way Artist Date Template*.

## Weeks

Start a week with the *Artist's Way Week Template* (Cmd+Shift+T, then pick it). Put the week's tasks in as checkboxes. A task that needs writing gets its own page from the *Artist's Way Exercise Template*, linked from the checkbox: the instructions at the top, your answer under **Response**. Tick the box in the week when it is done.

(If you have the book as an ebook, Claude can set up all twelve weeks from it for you. That copy stays private in your vault.)
